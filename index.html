Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Android package com.app.quotaisignals

This Privacy Policy explains how the Qyotex AI Signals mobile application (the "App") handles information when you use it. The App is operated by its independent developer ("we", "us", or "our"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Summary

The App does not require registration, does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and does not process payments. We do not operate our own analytics and we do not sell personal data. The App displays advertising provided by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, which receives limited technical information from your device in order to deliver ads. Ads are requested in non-personalized mode, and the App does not access the Android Advertising ID.

2. Information stored only on your device

The App may save a single display preference (whether full-screen immersive mode is enabled) in local storage on your device. This preference never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Information processed by the advertising partner

The App shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and may show a full-screen (interstitial) ad before opening the Telegram channel. These ads are delivered by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. To request, display, and measure ads, the SDK transmits technical information from your device to Yandex, which may include:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Device and app informationDevice model, operating system version, screen size, language, App versionDelivering ads in a format compatible with your device
Network informationIP address, connection typeDelivering ads and preventing ad fraud
Ad interaction dataAd impressions, clicks, and closesMeasuring ad performance

We have configured the SDK as follows:

  • Non-personalized ads. The SDK is told that the user has not consented to personalized advertising, so ads are not targeted based on a personal profile.
  • No Advertising ID. The Android permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is removed from the App, so the App does not provide the Google Advertising ID to the SDK.
  • No location. The App does not request location access and does not pass location data to the SDK.

All data transmitted by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Yandex processes this information as an independent controller under its own terms:

  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK Terms of Use

4. Telegram

The App contains links to the public Telegram channel https://t.me/AITrade_Signals. When you tap such a link, the channel opens in the Telegram app or your browser. The App does not send any personal information to Telegram. Your use of Telegram is governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

5. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or access:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • account credentials or payment and financial information;
  • your contacts, photos, videos, files, or messages;
  • your microphone, camera, or location.

6. How information is used

Technical information processed by the advertising partner is used only to display ads, measure ad performance, and prevent ad fraud. We do not use any information to build user profiles, and we do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Locally saved preferences remain on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Information received by Yandex is retained in accordance with the Yandex Privacy Policy. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
